# KPI and Reporting Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the key reporting requirements, operational metrics, and governance KPIs that the robotic surgery audit analytics system should support.

The reporting model is designed to simulate healthcare governance, operational oversight, and surgical audit reporting within an Irish healthcare environment.

---

# Operational Reporting Requirements

The system should support reporting on:

- Robotic surgery activity across hospitals
- Surgical volume trends over time
- Procedure activity by specialty
- Hospital-level robotic surgery utilisation
- Surgical approach comparisons
- Theatre activity and operating times
- Length of stay analysis
- Readmission and complication monitoring
- Governance and training compliance
- Programme maturity monitoring

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## 1. Total Robotic Surgery Volume

Measures the total number of robotic-assisted surgical procedures performed.

### Example Reporting:
- Cases per hospital
- Cases per specialty
- Monthly trends
- Annual trends

---

## 2. Surgical Approach Distribution

Measures the proportion of:
- Robotic surgery
- Laparoscopic surgery
- Open surgery

### Purpose:
Supports comparison of surgical approaches across hospitals and specialties.

---

## 3. Conversion to Open Surgery Rate

Measures the percentage of robotic procedures converted to open surgery.

### Formula:
(Number of converted cases / Total robotic cases) × 100

### Governance Relevance:
High conversion rates may indicate:
- case complexity,
- training issues,
- or programme maturity concerns.

---

## 4. Average Length of Stay

Measures average inpatient stay following surgery.

### Reporting Examples:
- By hospital
- By procedure
- By surgical approach

---

## 5. Complication Rate

Measures the percentage of surgical cases with recorded complications.

### Reporting Examples:
- By hospital
- By surgeon
- By specialty
- By procedure type

---

## 6. 30-Day Readmission Rate

Measures the percentage of patients readmitted within 30 days.

### Governance Relevance:
Important healthcare quality and patient outcome indicator.

---

## 7. Operating Time Analysis

Measures average operating theatre duration.

### Reporting Examples:
- By procedure
- By hospital
- By surgeon
- By robotic platform

---

## 8. Surgeon Governance Compliance

Measures completion of:
- robotic training,
- simulation training,
- governance review,
- and proctored case requirements.

### KPI Examples:
- Percentage compliant
- Outstanding governance reviews
- Training completion rates

---

## 9. Hospital Programme Maturity

Classifies hospitals according to robotic surgery programme development level.

### Example Categories:
- Emerging Programme
- Established Programme
- High-Volume Centre
- National Training Centre

---

## 10. Robotic Platform Utilisation

Measures utilisation of robotic surgery systems.

### Example Platforms:
- da Vinci Xi
- da Vinci X

### Reporting Examples:
- Cases per platform
- Procedure mix by platform
- Hospital platform utilisation

---

# Dashboard Requirements

The Power BI dashboard should support:

- Executive summary reporting
- Hospital comparisons
- Trend analysis over time
- Governance compliance monitoring
- Surgical outcomes monitoring
- Specialty-level analysis
- Interactive filtering and drill-down reporting

---

# Intended Reporting Audience

The reporting outputs are designed to simulate reporting requirements for:

- Hospital management teams
- Clinical governance groups
- Surgical programme leadership
- Healthcare operations teams
- Healthcare analysts
- National audit and quality improvement programmes

# Notes

All project data will be synthetic and anonymised.
