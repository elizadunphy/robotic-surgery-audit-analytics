# Project Objectives

## Background

Robotic-assisted surgery is becoming increasingly important within modern healthcare systems. Hospitals and healthcare organisations require accurate operational and governance reporting to monitor surgical activity, patient outcomes, training compliance, and performance trends.

This project simulates a healthcare audit and analytics system designed to support reporting and governance oversight for robotic-assisted surgical procedures.

The project is inspired by real-world healthcare operational reporting, clinical audit processes, and governance frameworks used within healthcare organisations.

---

## Business Problem

Healthcare organisations require structured data systems to:

- Monitor robotic surgery activity across hospitals
- Track surgical outcomes and complication rates
- Compare performance across surgical specialties
- Support governance and quality assurance reporting
- Monitor surgeon training and accreditation
- Analyse operational trends and patient outcomes
- Support evidence-based decision-making

Many healthcare environments rely on data from multiple disconnected systems, making reporting and governance oversight difficult.

This project demonstrates how a structured relational database and reporting solution could support operational visibility and healthcare analytics.

---

## Project Goals

The main goals of the project are:

1. Design a relational SQL database for robotic surgery audit data
2. Create realistic synthetic healthcare datasets
3. Develop SQL queries for operational and governance reporting
4. Build healthcare-focused KPI dashboards using Power BI
5. Demonstrate practical data analytics and reporting skills
6. Simulate a real-world healthcare audit environment

---

## Intended Users

The system is designed to simulate reporting requirements for:

- Hospital management teams
- Clinical governance teams
- Surgical programme managers
- Healthcare analysts
- Audit and quality improvement teams
- Healthcare operations management

---

## Planned Outputs

The project will include:
