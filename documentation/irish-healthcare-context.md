# Irish Healthcare Context

## Purpose

This document defines the Irish healthcare context used for the synthetic robotic surgery audit analytics project.

The project uses realistic Irish hospital names, surgical specialties, robotic surgery terminology, and governance concepts to simulate a healthcare audit and reporting environment.

---

## Hospitals Included

The synthetic dataset will include a selection of Irish hospitals associated with major surgical services and/or robotic surgery activity.

Planned hospitals include:

- St Vincent's University Hospital
- Beaumont Hospital
- Mater Misericordiae University Hospital
- Tallaght University Hospital
- University Hospital Limerick
- Cork University Hospital
- Galway University Hospitals

---

## Robotic Surgery Platforms

The project will use realistic robotic surgery platform names, including:

- da Vinci Xi
- da Vinci X

These platforms will be used in synthetic data for robotic-assisted surgical procedures.

---

## Surgical Specialties

The project will include specialties commonly associated with robotic-assisted surgery, including:

- Urology
- Colorectal Surgery
- Gynaecology
- General Surgery
- Thoracic Surgery
- ENT Surgery

---

## Example Procedures

Planned procedure examples include:

- Robotic prostatectomy
- Robotic partial nephrectomy
- Robotic hysterectomy
- Robotic colorectal resection
- Robotic hernia repair
- Robotic thoracic procedure
- Laparoscopic comparator procedures
- Open comparator procedures

---

## Programme Maturity Categories

Hospitals will be assigned a robotic surgery programme maturity category for reporting purposes:

- Emerging Programme
- Established Programme
- High-Volume Centre
- National Training Centre

---

## Governance Concepts

The project will include governance and training concepts such as:

- Robotic surgery training completion
- Simulation training
- Proctored case completion
- Governance review
- Audit review
- Programme oversight
- Surgeon credentialing
- Hospital programme maturity

---

## Data Use

All data used in this project will be synthetic.

No real patient data, real surgeon records, or confidential hospital information will be used.
